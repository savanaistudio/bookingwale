FAQ

Questions from clinics.

Can each doctor have different OPD timings?

Yes. Every doctor can follow the clinic hours or have their own days, morning and evening OPD and breaks. Leave is marked separately.

Can patients choose a doctor?

Yes. Patients can pick a specific doctor or take the earliest free slot with any doctor who offers that consultation. You can also turn doctor choice off.

How do I stop patients booking too close to the time?

Set a minimum notice, for example 60 minutes, and a maximum number of days ahead. The booking page only offers times inside those limits.

Can I ask for the reason for visit?

Yes. Add your own questions such as reason for visit, age or allergies, and make any of them required.

Can the clinic take an advance online?

Yes, you can collect an online advance for chosen services. Every online transaction is charged 2.5% + GST extra, outside your plan. Payments recorded at reception have no fee.
