FAQ

Questions from beauty parlours.

Can I block a full day for a bridal booking?

Yes. A bridal service has its own long duration, so it blocks the beautician for that whole time and no one else can book over it.

Can clients book quick services like threading?

Yes. Short services are listed with their time and price, and clients can add more than one service in a single booking.

Can I ask for skin type before the facial?

Yes. Add questions like skin type or allergies to the booking form.

Do reminders go on WhatsApp?

Yes. Clients get a confirmation and reminders on WhatsApp before the appointment.

Can I take an advance for bridal bookings?

Yes. Every online transaction is charged 2.5% + GST extra, outside your plan. Cash and UPI at the counter have no fee.
