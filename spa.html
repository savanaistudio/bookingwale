FAQ

Questions from spas.

Can I add cleanup time between sessions?

Yes. Each therapy can have a gap after it, so the therapist and room are free and ready for the next guest.

Can guests choose a therapist?

Yes, or take the earliest free time with any therapist who offers that therapy.

Can I limit late cancellations?

Yes. Set how long before the session a guest can still cancel online.

Can I ask health or pressure preferences?

Yes. Add your own questions to the booking form and make them required if needed.

Can guests pay online?

Yes, for therapies you choose. Every online transaction is charged 2.5% + GST extra, outside your plan. Payments at the desk have no fee.
