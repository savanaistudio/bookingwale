The daily struggle

Serving patients and clients is hard enough. Booking them shouldn't be.

Most clinics, salons, parlours and spas still book on calls, WhatsApp and a diary at the reception. It works, until the day gets busy.

Phone all day

Calls in the middle of a consultation

Every booking means stopping work, checking the diary and calling back.

Double bookings

Two people, one slot, same time

A slot given on WhatsApp and again at the reception. Someone waits, someone leaves.

No-shows

Empty slots you could have filled

Patients and clients forget. Without a reminder the hour is lost.

Scattered chats

Appointments buried in WhatsApp

Who booked what, with which doctor or stylist, at what time? Scrolling chats is not a system.

Staff confusion

Which doctor is in today?

Different hours, OPD days, leave and breaks for every doctor and staff member, kept in someone's head.

No clear picture

How did today actually go?

Visits, payments and new patients only add up at the end of the month, if at all.

FAQ

Questions owners ask us.

What exactly is BookingWale?

An online booking page for your patients or clients, plus an admin panel for you. They book free times themselves; you manage appointments, doctors or staff, hours and WhatsApp reminders in one place.

Does it work for clinics?

Yes. Clinics start with doctor and patient words, consultation and follow-up services, and questions like reason for visit. Each doctor can have their own OPD timings and leave.

Do patients need to install an app?

No. Your booking page opens in any phone browser from a link you share on WhatsApp, Instagram or Google.

Can each doctor or stylist have different hours?

Yes. Each one can follow the business hours or have their own, with leave days marked separately.

How do you stop double bookings?

A time is offered only if a doctor or staff member is free for the whole service, including gaps between appointments. The booking is checked again when it is saved, so two people cannot take the same slot.

How do reminders work?

Confirmations, changes, cancellations and reminders are sent on WhatsApp, a day before and a few hours before the appointment. You can edit the message text.

Are there charges on online payments?

Yes, as an extra that is not part of your plan price. Every online transaction (an advance or payment made through your booking page) is charged 2.5% + GST. If you don't take online payments, you pay nothing extra. Cash, UPI and card payments you record at the counter have no fee.

Which plan do I need?

Both plans include every feature. Starter lets you list up to 5 services for ₹1,000 a month. Unlimited lets you list as many as you want for ₹1,500 a month.

What happens after the free month?

You continue on Starter at ₹1,000 a month or Unlimited at ₹1,500 a month. On a yearly plan you pay for 11 months and get 12. If you don't continue, nothing is charged.

How long does setup take?

Usually a day. We enter your services, doctors or staff, hours and colours with you.

Will it look like my brand?

Yes. You choose the colours, logo, banner and tagline, and rename words like doctor, patient and appointment.
