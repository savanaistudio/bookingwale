FAQ

Questions from salons.

Can clients choose their stylist?

Yes. They can pick a stylist or take the earliest free time with anyone who does that service. You can turn stylist choice off.

What about long services like keratin or colour?

Each service has its own duration and cleanup gap, so a 2½ hour treatment blocks exactly 2½ hours on that stylist's chart.

Can a senior stylist charge more?

Yes. Set a different price or duration per stylist for the same service.

Do clients need an app?

No. The booking page opens in any phone browser from your Instagram bio, WhatsApp status or Google profile.

Can I take an advance for big bookings?

Yes, for services you choose. Every online transaction is charged 2.5% + GST extra, outside your plan. Cash and UPI at the counter have no fee.
